# PRODUCT.md - ViewMarket Platform

## Product Truth
- **Product Name**: ViewMarket
- **Core Value Proposition**: Non-custodial algorithmic trading software, workflow tooling, and computational analytics platform for Indian capital markets (NSE/BSE). Enables traders to visually author condition flowcharts, run isolated compute backtest sweeps, and dispatch click-to-trade orders directly to personal broker accounts (Zerodha Kite, Upstox, Dhan, Angel One) with zero server credential persistence and zero tick relay.
- **Target Audience**: Quantitative traders, retail market participants, algorithmic strategists, and systematic Indian equities/F&O investors.
- **Tone & Voice**: Disciplined, transparent, technically rigorous, non-promotional, strictly compliant with SEBI regulations.

## Surfaces & Modes
- **Surfaces**:
  - Landing Page (`/`): Persuade & Explain Non-Custodial Architecture
  - Legal & Trust Center (`/legal/*`): Compliance, Disclaimers, and Terms
- **Key Conversion Goals**:
  1. Primary CTA: "Open Strategy Studio"
  2. Secondary CTA: "Sign In", "Launch Studio"
  3. Trust & Authority: Direct client-to-broker WebSockets, mandatory click-to-trade confirmation, zero central token storage, 100% SEBI retail algo compliance.
